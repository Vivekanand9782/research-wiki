## Title & Metadata
* **Full Paper Title:** Characterization of DFR allelic variations and their associations with pre-harvest sprouting resistance in a set of red-grained Chinese wheat germplasm
* **Authors:** H. H. Bi, Y. W. Sun, Y. G. Xiao, L. Q. Xia
* **Year:** 2014 (Published online August 15, 2013)
* **Journal:** Euphytica
* **DOI:** 10.1007/s10681-013-0986-z

## Abstract Summary
[[Pre-harvest sprouting]] ([[PHS]]) severely reduces wheat grain quality. Red-grained wheat varieties are generally more resistant to [[PHS]] than white-grained ones, though some red varieties remain susceptible. This study characterized [[dihydroflavonol-4-reductase]] ([[TaDFR]]) gene variations in 120 red-grained Chinese wheat cultivars. While no functional variations were found in [[TaDFR-A]] or [[TaDFR-D]], a novel allele, [[TaDFR-Bb]], was identified on chromosome 3B. This allele features an 8 bp insertion (CTCTAGGA) in its promoter region. A [[CAPS marker]] was developed to detect this insertion. Wheat varieties carrying [[TaDFR-Bb]] had a significantly lower average [[germination index]] (23.6%) compared to those without it (69.5%), indicating higher [[PHS]] resistance. The insertion was associated with increased [[TaDFR-B]] expression in immature seeds. Transient expression of [[TaDFR-B]] in [[Chinese Spring]] coleoptiles did not independently induce [[anthocyanin]] synthesis.

## Introduction & Background
[[Pre-harvest sprouting]] ([[PHS]]) refers to the germination of grains on the ear before harvest, typically triggered by damp conditions. It leads to lower yields and reduced end-product quality due to the synthesis of [[alpha-amylase]], which breaks down starch and ruins baking properties. 

Red-grained wheat varieties are generally more tolerant to [[PHS]] than white-grained ones. The red pigment is synthesized via the [[flavonoid biosynthetic pathway]], which involves enzymes such as [[chalcone synthase]] ([[CHS]]), [[chalcone flavanone isomerase]] ([[CHI]]), [[flavanone 3-hydroxylase]] ([[F3H]]), and [[dihydroflavonol-4-reductase]] ([[DFR]]). These genes are expressed in the immature grain coat of red wheat but are suppressed in white wheat. 

The transcription factor [[Viviparous1]] ([[Vp1]]) coordinates grain color and [[PHS]] via Myb-dependent and Myb-independent pathways. [[Myb/c1]] transcription factors (such as [[Tamyb10]]), located on the long arms of chromosome 3, activate flavonoid genes. [[Vp1]] interacts with the Sph element of [[Myb/c1]], which in turn binds to the promoter of [[DFR]]. Although [[DFR]] is a key regulatory point upstream of anthocyanins, its direct role in [[PHS]] resistance in red-grained wheat has remained unclear. This study aims to characterize [[TaDFR]] allelic variations and their association with [[PHS]] resistance in Chinese wheat germplasm.

## Key Concepts & Theory
* **[[Pre-harvest sprouting]] ([[PHS]])**: Germination of wheat grains on the spike before harvest, triggered by wet weather, leading to high [[alpha-amylase]] activity and poor baking quality.
* **[[Flavonoid biosynthetic pathway]]**: The metabolic pathway responsible for synthesizing red pigments (such as [[anthocyanin]], [[proanthocyanidin]], and [[catechin]]) in the wheat seed coat.
* **[[Dihydroflavonol-4-reductase]] ([[DFR]])**: A key structural enzyme in the [[flavonoid biosynthetic pathway]] that catalyzes the reduction of dihydroflavonols.
* **[[Germination index]] ([[GI]])**: A quantitative measure of seed dormancy and [[PHS]] susceptibility, calculated based on daily germination counts over seven days.
* **[[CAPS marker]] (Cleaved Amplified Polymorphic Sequence)**: A molecular marker system combining PCR amplification and restriction enzyme digestion to detect specific genetic polymorphisms.
* **[[Seed dormancy]]**: An internal block that prevents intact viable seeds from germinating under favorable conditions, closely linked to red grain color in wheat.

## Important Entities
* **[[Triticum aestivum]]** (wheat): The plant species investigated.
* **[[Chinese Spring]]** (CS): The wheat cultivar used for transient expression assays.
* **[[TaDFR]]** ([[dihydroflavonol-4-reductase]]): The target gene family studied.
* **[[TaDFR-A]]**, **[[TaDFR-B]]**, **[[TaDFR-D]]**: Homoeologous genes located on chromosomes 3A, 3B, and 3D.
* **[[TaDFR-Ba]]**, **[[TaDFR-Bb]]**, **[[TaDFR-Bc]]**: Alleles of the [[TaDFR-B]] gene.
* **[[Tamyb10]]** / **[[Myb10-D]]**: Transcription factor regulating flavonoid biosynthesis.
* **[[Viviparous1]]** (**[[Vp1]]** / **[[TaVp-1D]]**): Transcription factor regulating seed maturation and dormancy.
* **[[Hinf I]]**: Restriction endonuclease used for the [[CAPS marker]] assay.
* **[[alpha-amylase]]**: Enzyme that breaks down starch during sprouting.
* **[[pAHC-PSK]]**: Plasmid vector used for transient expression assays.
* **[[Longmai 13]]**, **[[Kenjiu 1]]**, **[[Long 94-4081]]**, **[[Longfumai 10]]**, **[[Kefeng 10]]**, **[[Long 05-5061]]**: [[PHS]] resistant wheat cultivars.
* **[[Jilimai]]**, **[[Kefeng 9]]**, **[[Aiganzao]]**, **[[Yangmai 3]]**, **[[Hongtuzi]]**: Intermediate [[PHS]] response cultivars.
* **[[Taiyuan 566]]**, **[[Fengkang 8]]**, **[[Jingshuang 16]]**, **[[Nongda 45]]**, **[[Nongda 311]]**, **[[Jingdong 6]]**: [[PHS]] susceptible wheat cultivars.

## Methods & Experimental Design
* **Plant Materials**: 120 red-grained Chinese wheat cultivars/lines with distinct [[PHS]] responses were grown over two seasons (2000–2001 and 2001–2002) at two locations (Anyang and Beijing).
* **[[PHS]] Response Assay**: Spikes harvested at dough-yellow ripening (~40 DPA) were hand-threshed, sterilized, and germinated in Petri dishes. Daily germinated seed counts over 7 days were used to calculate the [[germination index]] ([[GI]]).
* **Gene Cloning**: Full genomic sequences of [[TaDFR-A]], [[TaDFR-B]], and [[TaDFR-D]] were amplified from 10 cultivars using genome-specific primers and sequenced.
* **[[CAPS Marker]] Development**: Primers DFR-B-F/R were designed to amplify the promoter region of [[TaDFR-B]]. The PCR product was digested with [[Hinf I]] restriction enzyme at 37°C for 3 hours and resolved on 1.5% agarose gels.
* **Expression Analysis**: Total RNA was extracted from immature grains at 5 and 10 DPA from seven cultivars. Quantitative real-time PCR ([[qRT-PCR]]) was performed using [[TaDFR-B]]-specific primers, with the wheat actin gene as an internal control.
* **Transient Expression Assay**: [[TaDFR-B]] and [[TaMyb10-D]] genes were cloned into the [[pAHC-PSK]] vector under the maize ubiquitin promoter. Gold particles coated with these constructs were delivered into [[Chinese Spring]] coleoptiles via particle bombardment. Coleoptiles were cultured on MS media in darkness for 24 hours to observe red pigmentation.

## Key Results & Data
* **Sequence Polymorphisms**: No polymorphisms were found in [[TaDFR-D]]. Polymorphisms in [[TaDFR-A]] showed no association with [[PHS]]. For [[TaDFR-B]], three alleles were identified: [[TaDFR-Ba]], [[TaDFR-Bb]] (containing an 8 bp CTCTAGGA insertion at position -282 to -275 bp in the promoter and a SNP at 443 bp in the first intron), and [[TaDFR-Bc]] (containing only the intron SNP).
* **Association with [[PHS]]**: The 8 bp insertion in [[TaDFR-Bb]] created a [[Hinf I]] restriction site. Screening the 120 cultivars using the [[CAPS marker]] revealed that 70 out of 71 [[PHS]] resistant cultivars, 13 out of 18 intermediate cultivars, and 17 out of 31 susceptible cultivars carried the [[TaDFR-Bb]] allele.
* **Statistical Significance**: Cultivars carrying the 8 bp insertion ([[TaDFR-Bb]]) had a mean [[germination index]] of 23.6%, which was significantly lower (P < 0.01) than the mean [[germination index]] of 69.5% for cultivars lacking the insertion.
* **Gene Expression**: [[qRT-PCR]] showed that [[TaDFR-B]] expression was higher at 5 DPA than at 10 DPA. Cultivars with the 8 bp insertion (e.g., [[Nongda 45]], [[Nongda 311]], [[Aiganzao]], [[Yangmai 3]]) showed higher [[TaDFR-B]] expression than corresponding cultivars without the insertion (e.g., [[Jingdong 6]], [[Hongtuzi]]). Among cultivars with the insertion, expression was highest in the highly resistant cultivar [[Long 05-5061]] and lowest in the susceptible cultivar [[Nongda 311]].
* **Transient Expression**: Bombardment of [[Chinese Spring]] coleoptiles with [[pAHC-PSK]]-Myb10 induced distinct red spots (pigmentation). In contrast, bombardment with [[pAHC-PSK]]-TaDFR did not produce any red pigmentation.

## Mechanistic Insights
* The 8 bp insertion (CTCTAGGA) in the promoter region of [[TaDFR-Bb]] does not reside within known regulatory elements (such as G-box, myb-binding, Dof, TACpyAT, or SBF-1 elements). However, it forms a repeat sequence with an adjacent sequence. The authors suggest that this repeat sequence might increase the expression level of [[TaDFR-Bb]] by altering the secondary structure of the upstream promoter region, thereby enhancing the binding of related transcription factors.
* A positive correlation was observed between [[TaDFR-B]] expression levels and [[PHS]] resistance among cultivars carrying the [[TaDFR-Bb]] allele.
* Transient expression assays demonstrated that overexpressing [[TaDFR-B]] alone is insufficient to trigger [[anthocyanin]] synthesis or red pigmentation in coleoptiles. This is because [[DFR]] acts downstream of three other enzymes ([[CHS]], [[CHI]], and [[F3H]]) in the [[flavonoid biosynthetic pathway]]. Without concurrent upregulation of these upstream enzymes, the substrates required for [[DFR]] activity are unavailable, preventing pigment formation.

## Conclusions & Implications
* The study successfully identified a novel allele, [[TaDFR-Bb]], characterized by an 8 bp promoter insertion that is strongly associated with higher [[PHS]] resistance in red-grained Chinese wheat.
* The insertion enhances [[TaDFR-B]] gene expression during early grain development (5 DPA).
* The developed [[CAPS marker]] provides a reliable tool for marker-assisted selection (MAS) in wheat breeding programs aiming to improve [[PHS]] resistance in red-grained wheat germplasm.

## Limitations & Caveats
* The association between the [[TaDFR-Bb]] allele and [[PHS]] resistance is not absolute; 17 [[PHS]] susceptible cultivars also carried the 8 bp insertion, and 1 resistant cultivar lacked it, indicating that other genetic factors or genes (such as [[Vp1]] or [[Tamyb10]]) also influence [[PHS]] susceptibility.
* The study does not provide direct functional proof of how the 8 bp insertion physically alters transcription factor binding or chromatin structure.
* The transient expression assay was performed in coleoptiles rather than the seed coat (testa), which is the primary site of [[PHS]]-related pigment synthesis.

## Future Directions
* Investigate the precise molecular mechanism by which the 8 bp promoter insertion increases [[TaDFR-B]] transcription, potentially through electrophoretic mobility shift assays (EMSA) or dual-luciferase reporter assays.
* Analyze the epistatic interactions between [[TaDFR-Bb]], [[Tamyb10]], and [[Vp1]] alleles to better understand the coordinated regulation of grain color and [[seed dormancy]].
* Validate the utility of the developed [[CAPS marker]] in diverse global wheat breeding populations.
